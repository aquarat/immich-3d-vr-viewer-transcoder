Immich VR connecting…

Stereoscopic and 360 media from your library, in the headset. Pick a collection, double-tap a clip, and put it on.

Viewing options

In the headset, with hands or controllers: the control panel sits under the video. Point and pinch a button to press it, or pinch the progress bar and slide to scrub. Pinch and drag the video, or the panel's background, to move it. The link button decides whether they move together (the default) or separately. Tap the video, or empty space, to play or pause; pinch and hold on empty space to leave, or use Exit VR. The Browse button (four squares) opens a pane of every collection: tap a thumbnail to play it, drag the grid or flick it to scroll, or grab the bar on its right edge to jump through a long list. Positions are remembered. Double-tap a thumbnail here to go straight in.
